# The Daily Indexing Ritual (30 seconds)

The single biggest free ranking accelerator. Do this every morning after the "Your articles are live" email arrives.

1. Open the email.
2. For each article URL listed, click its **"request indexing"** link. This opens Google Search Console's URL Inspection tool, pre-filled with that article's URL.
3. Click **Request Indexing** inside Google Search Console.
4. Repeat for each article in the email (Search Console limits you to roughly 10 requests a day per property, which is plenty at 5 articles a day).

That's it. It tells Google "this page exists, please look now" instead of waiting for Google to find it on its own, which can take days to weeks.

Skim the article titles while you're in there. If anything reads wrong, reply to yourself with a note or open Claude Code and say what to fix. No pre-approval was needed to publish, so it's on you to catch anything off after the fact, and this daily minute is when to do it.
