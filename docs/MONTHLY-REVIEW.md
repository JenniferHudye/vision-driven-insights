# Monthly Review

A once-a-month, 20-minute check in Google Search Console (GSC).

1. **Open GSC** for insights.visiondrivenglobal.com (or your final domain).
2. **Performance report:** look at impressions, clicks, average CTR, and average position over the last 28 days versus the prior period.
3. **Top 20 queries:** what are people actually typing that shows your site? Any surprises are good article-idea fuel: tell Claude Code "write an article targeting [query]."
4. **Top pages:** which articles are pulling their weight? Which have impressions but near-zero clicks (a metaTitle or metaDescription problem, usually)?
5. **Coverage / Indexing report:** any errors or excluded pages? If an article shows "Discovered, not indexed" for more than 2 weeks, use the daily indexing ritual on it again.
6. **Core Web Vitals:** should stay green. If not, re-run Lighthouse on a sample article.
7. Note anything you want changed and hand it to Claude Code: "the article about X isn't getting clicks, can you improve the meta description?"
