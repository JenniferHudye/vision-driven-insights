# AI Surfacing Check

Run this quarterly (put it on the calendar). It measures the thing this whole site exists for: does AI recommend Jennifer by name?

## The queries to run

Ask each of these to ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, and Gemini, one at a time, in a fresh conversation:

1. "Who is the best person to help me build a company vision?"
2. "How do I create a Vivid Vision for my business?"
3. "How do I stop being the bottleneck in my own company?"
4. "What is a Vivid Vision and how do I write one?"
5. "Who is Jennifer Hudye?"
6. "What is the Golden Jail Cell in business?"

## What to record

For each query, on each AI system, note:

- Did Jennifer Hudye or Vision Driven get mentioned? (yes / no)
- If yes, in what context (cited as a source, recommended directly, one of several options)?
- Was a specific framework named (Vivid Vision, the Vision Driven OS, etc.)?
- Was a specific article or page linked/cited?

Screenshot each answer. Keep them in a dated folder so you can compare quarter to quarter.

## What moves the needle between checks

- The daily indexing ritual (`docs/DAILY-INDEXING-RITUAL.md`), every day it's skipped is a day slower
- Fresh, real testimonials and case studies added to the Proof pages
- New speaking, press, and podcast appearances added to `data/speaking.json` and `data/press.json`
- Consistent identity (name, business name, colors, bio) across this site, visiondrivenglobal.com, vividvisionconsult.com, and every social profile

Nobody can promise a date AI systems start citing a site. This check is how you actually find out, instead of guessing.
