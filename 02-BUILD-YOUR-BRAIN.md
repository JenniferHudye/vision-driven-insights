# Build Your Brand "Brain"

Your site is only as good as the thinking behind it: who you are, who you serve, what you stand for, your named frameworks, and your proof. We call that your **brain**. Everything the build writes flows from it.

You have two ways to create it. Pick whichever you like, Claude (back in your build chat) will use the result either way.

---

## Option 1: The Brain Builder GPT (fastest, ~15 minutes) ✅ recommended

There's a custom GPT that interviews you and writes your brain for you.

1. Go to: **https://chatgpt.com/g/g-6a23348a2f448191af4f50c328f57989-ua-agent-ai-brain-builder**
2. When it asks for an access code to continue, enter: **`Top Producer`**
3. Answer its questions. It walks you through your positioning, your ideal client, your point of view, and your frameworks. Be specific and talk like yourself, the more real your answers, the better your site sounds like you.
4. When it's done, **copy everything it produced.**
5. Go back to your Claude build chat and paste it in. Tell Claude: "Here's my brain, turn it into my client brief."

That's it. Claude takes the brain and builds your `client-brief.md` from it, asking you only for anything the brain didn't cover.

> No ChatGPT account, or you'd rather not use it? Use Option 2 instead, you lose nothing.

---

## Option 2: Let Claude interview you directly

If you'd rather not leave Claude, it can interview you right where you are.

In your build chat, just say: **"Interview me to build my brief."** Claude will run the guided intake (`Stage-1-Intake-Prompt.md`). It even does a quick research sweep on you online first to pre-fill what it can, then asks you to confirm and add the rest. Takes 30-45 minutes and writes your `client-brief.md` directly.

---

## What a strong brain needs (either way)

Whichever path you choose, the build is only as strong as these. Have them in mind:

- **Who you serve**, one specific ideal client, not "everyone."
- **Your distinctive point of view**, the thing you believe that most experts in your space get wrong. One clean sentence.
- **At least 2 named frameworks**, your signature methods, given names. Even if you've never named them publicly, name them now. This is what AI cites.
- **Your proof**, testimonials with real names and outcomes, case studies, awards, press, speaking, books. The more specific, the more AI trusts you.
- **Your voice**, phrases you actually say, and words you'd never use.

Don't have all of it polished? Fine. The GPT and Claude will both help you crystallize it. The point is to get it on paper, that's the foundation everything else stands on.
