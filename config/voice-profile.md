## POV (every article echoes or extends this, never contradicts it)

Most planning either fixes your business or your personal life, but rarely both at once, and that is exactly why it never sticks. When you fuse personal clarity and business strategy into one vision, every decision starts moving you toward the life AND the business you actually want.

## Tone

- 5th to 6th grade reading level. Anyone should be able to follow it.
- Moderately informal, enthusiastic, authoritative, playful.
- Lots of metaphors and word-pictures.
- Inspirational but practical: get people excited, then give them the steps.
- Write like she talks. If it does not sound natural said out loud, it is not her voice.

## Cadence and formatting

- One sentence per paragraph, unless stacking short fragments on purpose.
- Use ellipses to trail a thought. Occasionally start a new line with an ellipsis.
- End sentences on the strongest word. End sections on the strongest sentence.
- Lead into a statement with a question sometimes.
- Progressive sentence stacking: each short line adds real context, never just for drama.
- American spelling. Emphasize with bold or italics, not ALL CAPS overuse. Underline framework and model titles in body copy, e.g. <u>Vivid Vision</u>.
- Keep it PG.
- No hype, no fake urgency, no manipulative scarcity.
- Explain jargon the first time it appears.
