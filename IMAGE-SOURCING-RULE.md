# Image Sourcing: copyright-safe images only (HARD RULE)

> This rule travels with the kit so your site stays legally clean even though it's built outside any larger system. It applies to every image on the site you build. Copyright, trademark, and likeness violations create real legal and financial exposure. When in doubt: don't use it, generate a clean original or use the branded placeholder instead.

---

## Why "royalty-free" is not enough

A free or royalty-free license (Unsplash / Pexels / Pixabay) clears the photo's **copyright**. It does NOT clear three other things that are just as actionable:

1. **A recognizable person's face**, right of publicity. These stock sites do not collect model releases; that risk is on you.
2. **A logo, brand name, or branded product in the shot**, trademark.
3. **Recognizable private property, artwork, characters, or famous landmarks**, separate IP.

So the rule isn't just "use approved sources." It's "use approved sources AND screen every image for faces, logos, and brands."

---

## Approved sources (the ONLY places images may come from)

### A. Free stock photos (commercial use, no attribution)
- **Unsplash** (unsplash.com)
- **Pexels** (pexels.com)
- **Pixabay** (pixabay.com)

On Pixabay, ignore the sponsored iStock/Shutterstock results at the top, those are paid ads. Only use results under the Pixabay Content License.

### B. AI-generated images (FREE tools only, no paid plans)
Use only free tools whose FREE tier grants commercial use. Generate only when no stock photo fits.

1. **Google Gemini / Imagen (free), default for AI.** Commercial use allowed on the free tier; Google claims no ownership. Carries an invisible SynthID marker that does not affect use.
2. **ChatGPT image (free).** OpenAI grants you ownership and commercial use of images you create, on every tier including free.
3. **Adobe Firefly (free).** Safest by training (licensed + public-domain only). Two catches on the free tier: it may add a watermark, and it carries no IP indemnification. Fine to use, just confirm the image is watermark-free before publishing.

**Do NOT use** Midjourney or Ideogram (commercial rights gated behind a paid plan, their free output is not safe to use commercially) or **Flux** (its "dev" model is non-commercial-only).

None of these free tools indemnify you. The face/logo/brand screen below is your real protection, run it on every image.

### C. Owned assets (always allowed)
- Photos you took or own.
- Your own headshot / branding photos.
- Charts, framework diagrams, and graphics you built yourself (Gamma, Figma, Canva).

---

## The screen: run on EVERY image before it ships

Reject the image if ANY answer is yes (unless it's an owned asset per section C):

- Recognizable human face? → REJECT
- Visible logo, brand name, or branded product? → REJECT
- Visible artwork, poster, or recognizable character? → REJECT
- Famous landmark or recognizable private building? → REJECT

Prefer: empty interiors, exteriors without signage, workspaces, keys, documents, laptops, abstract/lifestyle shots with no identifiable people, and your own branded diagrams.

---

## When generating with AI: never prompt for

- A named living artist's style ("in the style of [artist]")
- A real celebrity, public figure, or any specific real person
- A copyrighted character (Disney, Marvel, cartoons, mascots)
- A real brand's logo or trademarked product

Keep prompts generic: "modern bright office, no signage, no people."

---

## NEVER allowed (any source, any time)

- Any image scraped from Google Images, Pinterest, a competitor site, a news article, or the open web.
- Getty Images, Shutterstock free tier, or anything requiring attribution.
- Any image whose license can't be verified.

---

## When in doubt

If you are not 100% certain an image is clear on all of the above, do NOT use it. Generate a clean original (free Gemini or ChatGPT), or fall back to the branded placeholder the build generates automatically. Safe-but-plain always beats risky-but-pretty.
